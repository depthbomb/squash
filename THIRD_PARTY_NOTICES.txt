Squash can download the latest Gyan.dev FFmpeg Essentials build for Windows.
The archive is verified against the build provider's matching SHA-256 file
before extraction.

That build enables GPL components, including libx264 and libx265, and is
distributed under the GNU General Public License (GPL) version 3 or later.

Build information, checksums, the current FFmpeg source revision, external-
library versions, and source links:
https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/

FFmpeg license and compliance information:
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

The Essentials build may also contain permissively licensed libraries,
including libaom. Applicable notices and license texts are included in the
downloaded FFmpeg package.
